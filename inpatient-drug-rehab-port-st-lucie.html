Medical Detox and 24/7 Care

Medical detoxification represents the first crucial step in the recovery process for most individuals entering inpatient treatment. Our medical detox Port St Lucie services provide safe, supervised withdrawal from drugs and alcohol under the guidance of experienced medical professionals. This process can be physically and emotionally challenging, but proper medical supervision significantly reduces risks and discomfort.

⚠️ Important Safety Notice

Never attempt to detox from alcohol or benzodiazepines without medical supervision. Withdrawal from these substances can be life-threatening. Our 24/7 medical care ensures your safety throughout the detox process.

Our 24/7 inpatient care ensures that patients receive immediate attention if complications arise during detox or any other phase of treatment. Registered nurses are available around the clock to monitor vital signs, administer medications, and provide comfort measures. This continuous medical supervision is particularly important for individuals withdrawing from alcohol, benzodiazepines, or other substances that can cause potentially life-threatening withdrawal symptoms.

The detoxification process typically lasts three to seven days, depending on the substances used, duration of use, and individual factors. Our medical team may prescribe medications to ease withdrawal symptoms and prevent complications. These medications are carefully monitored and gradually reduced as the body adjusts to functioning without substances.

Beyond physical detox, our 24-hour care model provides emotional support during this vulnerable time. Many individuals experience anxiety, depression, or other emotional challenges during withdrawal. Our staff is trained to recognize these issues and provide appropriate interventions to ensure patient safety and comfort.

Family counseling recognizes that addiction affects entire family systems. Individual therapy sessions provide personalized attention to address specific issues such as trauma, mental health conditions, or personal challenges. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is inpatient drug rehab?

Inpatient drug rehabilitation is a comprehensive treatment program where individuals live at a treatment facility while receiving intensive therapy, medical care, and support for addiction recovery. Unlike outpatient treatment, inpatient rehab provides 24-hour supervision and care in a structured, substance-free environment.

How long does inpatient treatment last?

Treatment length varies based on individual needs, but most inpatient programs last 30, 60, or 90 days. Some individuals may benefit from longer treatment periods, particularly those with severe addictions or co-occurring mental health conditions. Our clinical team regularly assesses progress and makes recommendations about appropriate treatment duration.

Is medical detox included in inpatient rehab?

Yes, medical detoxification is typically the first phase of inpatient treatment for individuals who need supervised withdrawal from drugs or alcohol. Our medical team provides 24-hour monitoring and medication-assisted detox services to ensure safety and comfort during this process.

What insurance plans do you accept?

We accept most major insurance plans including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, and many others. Our financial counselors verify insurance benefits and work directly with insurance companies to maximize coverage for treatment services.

How do I start the admission process?

The admission process begins with a confidential phone call to our admission specialists. During this conversation, we gather information about treatment needs, assess eligibility, verify insurance coverage, and schedule comprehensive evaluations. We can often arrange same-day or next-day admission for individuals who need immediate treatment.

Can I bring personal items to treatment?

Yes, patients can bring certain personal items including comfortable clothing, toiletries, and items that provide comfort during treatment. We provide a detailed list of approved and prohibited items during the admission process. All belongings are searched upon arrival to ensure facility safety and security.

Do you treat co-occurring mental health conditions?

Yes, many individuals with addiction also have mental health conditions such as depression, anxiety, PTSD, or bipolar disorder. Our treatment programs address both addiction and mental health conditions through integrated treatment approaches and specialized therapies.

What happens if I relapse after treatment?

Relapse is sometimes part of the recovery process, and it doesn't mean treatment has failed. We provide ongoing support through alumni programs and can help arrange additional treatment if needed. Many individuals require multiple treatment episodes before achieving lasting sobriety, and we support patients through their entire recovery journey.
