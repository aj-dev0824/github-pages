Inpatient Addiction Treatment

Our inpatient addiction treatment Wellington program provides the highest level of care for individuals with severe addiction or those who have struggled with multiple failed attempts at recovery. This residential rehab option offers a structured, supportive environment where clients can focus entirely on their healing without the distractions and triggers of everyday life. Clients reside at our facility for the duration of their treatment, typically ranging from 30 to 90 days, depending on individual needs and progress.

The inpatient experience begins with a comprehensive assessment that evaluates not only the extent of addiction but also any co-occurring mental health conditions, physical health concerns, and psychosocial factors that may impact recovery. This thorough evaluation allows our clinical team to develop a personalized treatment plan that addresses all aspects of each client's unique situation.

Daily programming in our residential rehab includes individual therapy sessions, group counseling, educational workshops, life skills training, and recreational activities designed to promote physical and emotional well-being. One of the primary advantages of inpatient care is the 24/7 medical and clinical support available to address any complications or challenges that arise during early recovery. Outpatient Addiction Treatment

Our outpatient addiction treatment Wellington programs offer flexibility for individuals who need professional addiction care while maintaining their work, family, or educational responsibilities. These programs are ideal for people with mild to moderate addiction, those stepping down from inpatient care, or individuals who have strong support systems at home. Our outpatient options include traditional outpatient therapy, intensive outpatient programs (IOP), and partial hospitalization program services.

The traditional outpatient program involves scheduled therapy sessions and group meetings while allowing clients to live at home and continue with their daily responsibilities. This option works well for individuals with stable housing, supportive family relationships, and lower risk of relapse. Sessions typically occur several times per week and include individual counseling, group therapy, and family sessions when appropriate.

Our intensive outpatient program provides more structured support for individuals who need additional accountability and therapeutic intervention. Clients attend programming several days per week for multiple hours per day while still maintaining the ability to sleep at home and attend to essential responsibilities. This program bridges the gap between residential treatment and traditional outpatient care.

The partial hospitalization program offers the most intensive level of outpatient care, with clients attending treatment for most of the day, several days per week. This option provides comprehensive clinical services similar to inpatient care but allows individuals to return home each evening. It's particularly beneficial for people who need intensive support but have compelling reasons to remain in their home environment.

Detox Services

Safe, medically supervised detoxification is often the first step in the recovery journey, and our detox services Wellington provide compassionate care during this critical phase. Withdrawal from alcohol and drugs can be physically dangerous and emotionally challenging, which is why our medical team closely monitors all clients throughout the detox process. Our goal is to ensure safety and comfort while helping individuals successfully navigate withdrawal symptoms.

⚠️ Detox Safety Important

Never attempt to detox alone from alcohol, benzodiazepines, or opioids. These substances can cause life-threatening withdrawal complications. Professional medical supervision is essential for safety.

Our alcohol detox Wellington program addresses the specific medical needs of individuals withdrawing from alcohol. Alcohol withdrawal can be life-threatening in severe cases, with complications such as seizures and delirium tremens requiring immediate medical attention. Our medical staff is trained to recognize and treat these complications while providing medications when appropriate to manage withdrawal symptoms safely.

Drug detox Wellington services address withdrawal from various substances including opioids, stimulants, benzodiazepines, and other controlled substances. Each type of drug withdrawal presents unique challenges and timelines, and our medical team adapts care protocols accordingly. For opioid withdrawal, we may utilize medication-assisted treatment options to reduce cravings and withdrawal discomfort, while stimulant withdrawal typically focuses on managing psychological symptoms and ensuring proper nutrition and rest.

Throughout the detox process, clients receive emotional support through individual counseling and group sessions designed to help them understand the recovery process and prepare for ongoing treatment. We believe that detox is not just about physical stabilization but also about beginning the psychological work of recovery. Our clinical team helps clients develop coping strategies for managing cravings and stress while building motivation for continued treatment.

Holistic and Therapeutic Approaches

Our holistic addiction treatment philosophy recognizes that lasting recovery requires addressing the whole person, not just the addiction. We integrate traditional evidence-based therapies with complementary approaches that promote physical, emotional, and spiritual well-being. This comprehensive approach helps clients develop a full toolkit of recovery skills while exploring different pathways to healing.

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT): Identify and change thought patterns and behaviors contributing to addiction
  • Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT): Develop emotional regulation and distress tolerance skills
  • Trauma-Informed Care: Address underlying trauma through EMDR and somatic therapies
  • Family Therapy: Repair relationships and establish healthy family dynamics
  • Group Therapy: Build peer support and practice interpersonal skills
  • Mindfulness & Meditation: Develop present-moment awareness and stress management
  • Art & Music Therapy: Express emotions creatively and process experiences
  • Recreational Therapy: Develop healthy leisure activities and physical wellness

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) forms the foundation of our therapeutic programming, helping clients identify and change thought patterns and behaviors that contribute to addictive behaviors. Through CBT, clients learn to recognize triggers, challenge negative thinking, develop coping strategies, and build problem-solving skills that support long-term recovery. Our therapists work individually with clients to address their specific challenges and goals within the CBT framework.

Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) is particularly beneficial for clients who struggle with emotional regulation, relationship difficulties, or co-occurring mental health conditions such as borderline personality disorder. DBT teaches practical skills for managing intense emotions, improving interpersonal relationships, tolerating distress, and maintaining mindful awareness. These skills are invaluable for individuals whose addiction stems from attempts to cope with overwhelming emotional experiences.

Our therapy for addiction Wellington includes specialized approaches such as trauma-informed care, which recognizes the significant role that traumatic experiences play in the development and maintenance of addiction. Many individuals with substance use disorders have histories of physical, emotional, or sexual trauma, and addressing these underlying wounds is essential for sustainable recovery. Our trauma-informed therapies include EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing), somatic experiencing, and specialized group therapy for trauma survivors.

Family therapy plays a crucial role in our treatment programming, helping clients repair damaged relationships and establish healthier family dynamics. Addiction affects entire family systems, often leading to patterns of enabling, codependency, and broken trust. Our family therapists work with clients and their loved ones to improve communication, set healthy boundaries, and develop recovery-supportive relationships.

Creative and experiential therapies complement our traditional talk therapy approaches by providing alternative ways for clients to express themselves, process emotions, and develop new perspectives. Art therapy, music therapy, recreational therapy, and adventure therapy all offer unique benefits for different individuals. These approaches can be particularly helpful for clients who struggle with verbal expression or who find traditional therapy approaches insufficient on their own.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What types of addiction do you treat?

We provide comprehensive treatment for all types of substance use disorders, including alcohol addiction, prescription drug dependency (opioids, benzodiazepines, stimulants), illegal drug addiction (cocaine, heroin, methamphetamine, marijuana), and polysubstance abuse. Our clinical team has extensive experience treating complex addiction cases, including individuals with co-occurring mental health disorders such as depression, anxiety, PTSD, and bipolar disorder. We also specialize in treating professionals, healthcare workers, and individuals from specific populations who may have unique treatment needs.

How long is the typical treatment program?

Treatment length varies significantly based on individual needs, addiction severity, and progress in recovery. Inpatient programs typically range from 30 to 90 days, with many clients benefiting from 60-day stays that allow sufficient time for thorough assessment, stabilization, and skill development. Outpatient programs may continue for several months to a year or more, with session frequency decreasing as clients demonstrate sustained progress. Our clinical team conducts regular assessments to determine appropriate treatment duration and will work with you to develop a plan that maximizes your chances of long-term success.

Do you offer family support services?

Family involvement is a cornerstone of our treatment philosophy, and we offer comprehensive family support services as part of our programming. These services include family therapy sessions, educational workshops about addiction and recovery, support groups for family members, and family weekend programs that bring clients and their loved ones together for healing activities. We recognize that addiction affects entire family systems, and involving family members in treatment significantly improves outcomes for everyone involved.

Is medical detox necessary before rehab?

Medical detox is recommended for most individuals entering treatment, particularly those who have been using alcohol, benzodiazepines, or opioids regularly. These substances can cause dangerous withdrawal symptoms that require medical supervision and, in some cases, medication management. Our medical team conducts a thorough assessment to determine whether detox is necessary and provides safe, comfortable detox services on-site. Even for substances that don't require medical detox, the supervised withdrawal process helps ensure safety and provides immediate clinical support as you begin your recovery journey.

What insurance plans do you accept?

We accept most major insurance plans including Aetna, BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna, United Healthcare, Humana, Medicare, and many regional and employer-sponsored plans. Our admissions team will verify your specific benefits and provide detailed information about covered services and any out-of-pocket costs. We also work with insurance companies to obtain pre-authorization when required and can assist with appeals if initial coverage requests are denied. For uninsured individuals, we offer payment plans and can provide information about financial assistance programs that may be available.

What happens after I complete treatment?

Aftercare planning is an essential component of our treatment programs, and we begin developing your discharge plan from the day you arrive. Upon completion of our program, clients typically transition to lower levels of care such as outpatient therapy, participate in community support groups like AA or NA, may move to sober living facilities, and maintain contact with our alumni services. We provide comprehensive relapse prevention planning, referrals to community resources, and ongoing support to help you maintain your recovery long-term.

Can I visit or communicate with family during treatment?

Yes, we encourage family involvement and communication throughout treatment, though specific policies vary depending on your program and phase of treatment. Most programs include scheduled family visiting hours, family therapy sessions, and opportunities for phone calls and video chats. We may restrict contact during the initial stabilization phase to help you focus on your recovery, but our goal is to involve family members in supportive ways throughout your treatment journey.

What should I bring to treatment?

We provide a comprehensive packing list during the admission process, but generally, you should bring comfortable clothing for daily activities and exercise, personal toiletries (alcohol-free products only), any prescribed medications in original containers, important documents like ID and insurance cards, and perhaps photos or inspirational items from home. Do not bring valuables, excessive amounts of cash, alcohol-based products, or any non-prescribed medications or supplements. Our admissions team will provide specific guidance based on your program.
