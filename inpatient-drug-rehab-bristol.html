Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is inpatient drug rehab?

Inpatient drug rehabilitation is a comprehensive treatment program where patients live at a residential facility for 30-90 days or longer, receiving 24/7 medical supervision, therapy, and support services to overcome substance use disorders. This intensive approach provides the highest level of care available for addiction treatment.

How long does inpatient rehab last?

Program length varies based on individual needs, substance involved, and treatment progress. Most programs range from 30-90 days, though some patients benefit from extended stays of 6 months or longer. Research shows that longer treatment durations generally correlate with better long-term outcomes.

Does insurance cover inpatient drug rehab?

Most insurance plans provide coverage for medically necessary addiction treatment. Coverage levels vary, but many policies cover 60-80% of inpatient treatment costs after deductibles are met. The Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act requires insurance companies to provide addiction treatment benefits comparable to other medical conditions.

What substances does your inpatient program treat?

Our program treats addiction to all substances, including alcohol, opioids, cocaine, methamphetamine, marijuana, prescription drugs, and polysubstance use disorders. We also address behavioral addictions and co-occurring mental health conditions such as depression, anxiety, and trauma-related disorders.

What can I bring to inpatient rehab?

Patients can bring personal clothing, toiletries, medications (which will be managed by medical staff), books, and approved personal items. A detailed packing list is provided prior to admission, and prohibited items include electronics, substances, and items that could pose safety risks. All belongings are searched upon admission for safety purposes.

How soon can I enter the program?

Admission timelines depend on bed availability and insurance authorization requirements. Emergency admissions can often be accommodated within 24-48 hours, while planned admissions may require several days to complete assessments and authorizations. Our admissions team works to expedite the process for individuals in crisis.
